About / How to use

This page turns a GeoJSON boundary into a 3D-printable model and lets you download STL or 3MF. It fills every island base, preserves community walls, and adds your selected print supports at base height.

  • Edit parameters (URL, simplification, heights and widths).
  • Click Create to apply parameter edits. Island connections rebuild immediately; pending parameter edits apply together with the switch.
  • Use mouse/touch in the 3D view: drag to orbit, scroll/pinch to zoom.
  • Download STL as one unified solid, or 3MF with named Base and Walls parts aligned in one assembly for slicer assignments. No colors or materials are assigned by the file.
